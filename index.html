Playful Relating

Playful Relating is about the explorative practice of open and mindful togetherness. A range of communication games serve as frames for varied encounters, practicing our listening and self-perception in contact with others.

Exploratory conversation spaces of this kind can have great potential for dialogue and lively coexistence. Not only in artistic processes, but in everyday interactions.

The Why

Playful Relating can touch on our lives in many different ways. We don’t want to impose a fixed idea for why to do them. Instead, we want to invite everyone to find their own why. That being said, here are two basic ideas that may serve as a starting point.
